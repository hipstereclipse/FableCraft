# Fable TLC Visual Reference Notes

Use these notes for 2005 Fable: The Lost Chapters visual/design reconstruction. Treat them as visual implementation guidance unless separately backed by gameplay data.

## Core Look

- [C] Target the original 2005 TLC style: low-poly, flat/painterly textures, exaggerated storybook-cartoon proportions, oversized heads/hands, and muted earth-tone palettes.
- [B] Reserve saturated identity colors for major signals: Guild blue/gold, Lady Grey purple, Jack crimson/white mask, Scythe gold/blue, wasp green/red/blue, Succubus purple.
- [B] Prefer chunky silhouettes and simple readable materials over smooth Anniversary-style detail.

## Demon Doors And Stone Faces

- [C] Demon Doors are large weathered grey carved-stone faces of a bald, strong-looking man with an unkempt beard, set into rock/walls.
- [C] TLC Demon Doors animate eyes and mouth when speaking; do not use later Fable II/III full-hair/neat-beard variants for TLC.
- [C] Examples: Heroes' Guild Door wants light, Knothole Glade Door opens when shot in the face, Rose Cottage Door opens for a gift, Greatwood Caves Door wants combat prowess.
- [B] Reuse a living-stone visual language for Demon Doors, Oracle-like faces, trolls, and old ruin rewards.

## Map And Ambient Detail

- [C] Bowerstone South includes tavern, general store, clothing/armor shop, blacksmith, barber, school, roaming chickens, poor shed-like houses; Bowerstone North is wealthy and refined.
- [C] Traders have backpack, distinctive hat, and exaggerated moustache; trader escorts travel through Greatwood/Darkwood-style routes.
- [C] Chickens are common in Oakvale/Bowerstone and are small white/brown farmyard animals; cows and livestock are ambient background animals.
- [B] Scatter tavern/shop/school/forge silhouettes, trader camps, chickens, abandoned shrines, and ruin markers as map-interest anchors.

## Rewards And Quest Props

- [C] TLC reward language includes legendary weapons, Demon Door rewards, trophies, outfit pieces, donations/sacrifices, silver keys/chests, and contest headgear.
- [B] For RTS/minigame implementations, convert these into readable rewards: resources, forge levels, temporary scouting, unit buffs, or unlock hints.
