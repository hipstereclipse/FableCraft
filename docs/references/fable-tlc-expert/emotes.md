# Every Emote / Expression in Fable: The Lost Chapters (2005)

## TL;DR
- Fable: The Lost Chapters has **31 player "expressions"** (emotes/actions), enumerated across the five tables of the Fable Wiki "List of Fable Expressions": 5 Social, 6 fame/renown, 11 alignment, 2 stealth, and **7 added by The Lost Chapters** (the four Snowspire "Oracle" gestures, Ballet Dance, Cock-A-Doodle-Doo, and Insult).
- Unlike the sequels, expressions in the first game are **almost never bought from a shop with "manuals"** — they are earned automatically by gaining renown, shifting your good/evil alignment, leveling your Guile skill, or completing specific Lost Chapters quests. (The "expression manual/vendor" idea is a Fable II/III feature and a fan mod, not part of the base TLC game.)
- Functionally, expressions manipulate three NPC opinion ratings — **love/hate, frightening/funny, and ugly/attractive** — plus the Hero's good/evil alignment: friendly gestures (Flirt, Apologise, Thanks) raise love and good alignment, scary ones (Sneer, Scary Laugh, Blood Lust Roar) raise fear, rude ones (Vulgar Thrust, Middle Finger, Insult) raise evil, and comedic ones (Belch, Fart, dances) raise the "funny" rating.

## Key Findings
- **How expressions are acquired in TLC:** at the start of the game; by reaching **Renown Ranks 1–5**; by reaching **good or evil alignment thresholds** (slightly/moderately/extremely); by raising the **Guile** skill (ranks 3 and 6); and via **Lost Chapters quests** (Oracle of Snowspire; Chicken Kicking Competition). If you don't unlock everything during play, the remaining expressions are granted after defeating the final boss and sitting through the credits.
- **The Lost Chapters added seven expressions** to the original game's roster: Yeron, Moryk, Calran, and Avisto (the four Snowspire Oracle gestures), Ballet Dance, Cock-A-Doodle-Doo, and Insult.
- **Mechanics:** Expressions are extendable — hold the button and a meter fills; releasing in the green "sweet spot" gives the best reaction. Missing the meter causes an embarrassing failure (e.g., soiling your breeches on a failed Fart/Belch), which raises "ugliness." A failed scary expression makes the NPC find you funny instead of fearful.
- **Categorization:** The in-game and wiki categories are Social, Renown (fame), Alignment, and Stealth/Guile. Functionally they map onto "good/friendly," "scary/evil," "rude/vulgar," "funny," and "romantic/seductive" types.

## Details

### Full roster at a glance (31 expressions)
Per the Fable Wiki "List of Fable Expressions": **5 Social** (Belch, Fart, Follow, Wait, Giggle); **6 fame** (Blood Lust Roar, Flirt, Manly Arm Pump, Scary Laugh, Sexy Hero Pose, Sneer); **11 alignment** (Air Guitar, Apologise, Cossack Dance, Crotch Grab, Disco Dance, Flamenco Dance, Kiss My Ass, Middle Finger, Tap Dance, Thanks, Vulgar Thrust); **2 stealth** (Lockpick, Steal); and **7 Lost Chapters additions** (Avisto, Calran, Moryk, Yeron, Ballet Dance, Cock-A-Doodle-Doo, Insult).

### Social Expressions (available at the beginning of the game)
These are the basic starting emotes every Hero has from childhood/the start.
- **Belch** — Available from start. Comedic/"fun" gesture that entertains NPCs and raises the "funny" rating. Note: failing the extended Belch increases your "ugliness" to villagers. Also usable to drive off the bully in the opening Lookout Point sequence.
- **Fart** — Available from start. Comedic gesture; makes NPCs laugh. Failing the extended Fart soils the Hero and increases "ugliness." (Together with Belch these are the "fart-and-belch" expressions.)
- **Follow** — Available from start. Utility: makes a targeted NPC follow the Hero (used to lead marriage candidates, lead villagers to sacrifice at the Chapel of Skorm, or recruit followers for fist-fights).
- **Wait** — Available from start. Utility: makes a following NPC stop and stay put.
- **Giggle** — Available from start. Light social/funny gesture.
- **Flirt** — Available from start (grouped with the fame expressions on some lists). The core courtship expression: raises an NPC's love/disposition and grows the heart icon needed for marriage and sex. Friendly → good-leaning.
- **Cossack Dance** — Available from start. A "fun" dance that makes NPCs laugh/raises the funny rating.

### Renown / Fame Expressions (unlocked by gaining renown)
Unlocked automatically as the Hero's renown rank increases.
- **Sneer** — Renown Rank 1. Scary-category gesture; increases how feared the Hero is.
- **Manly Arm Pump** — Renown Rank 2. Flirty/courtship gesture used (with Flirt and Sexy Hero Pose) to raise an NPC's attraction/love and grow the marriage heart.
- **Scary Laugh** — Renown Rank 3. Scary gesture; raises fear so villagers are more likely to flee.
- **Sexy Hero Pose** — Renown Rank 4. Flirty/courtship gesture; raises attractiveness/love. Considered one of the most useful early expressions for romance.
- **Blood Lust Roar** — Renown Rank 5. The strongest intimidation roar; raises scariness and makes NPCs run. A failed roar instead makes the target find you funny.

### Alignment Expressions (unlocked by good/evil alignment)
Because some require extreme good and others extreme evil, collecting them all requires playing both a very good and a very evil character at different times.
- **Apologise** — Slightly Good. Social/good gesture; notably, if used after committing a crime (to the reporting villager or a guard), non-violent crimes are forgiven and fines dropped.
- **Air Guitar** — Slightly Evil. Fun/rude-leaning gesture.
- **Thanks** — Moderately Good. Friendly/social, good-aligned gesture.
- **Flamenco Dance** — Moderately Good. Good-aligned "fun" dance.
- **Middle Finger** — Moderately Evil. Rude/offensive gesture; gains evil points and worsens NPC opinion.
- **Crotch Grab** — Extremely Good. (Despite being crude, the wiki lists it under extremely good alignment.)
- **Disco Dance** — Extremely Good. Good-aligned "fun" dance.
- **Vulgar Thrust** — Extremely Evil. Rude/evil pelvic-thrust gesture; gains evil points and provokes negative reactions. Performing it while naked on a villager will get you reported to the guards.
- **Kiss My Ass** — Available at multiple alignments. Rude/offensive gesture.
- **Tap Dance** — Available at multiple alignments. "Fun" dance.

### Guile / Stealth Expressions (unlocked by leveling Guile)
- **Steal** — Learned at **Guile Level 3, which costs 2,200 experience** (per the Steam Community "Fable TLC Experience Guide": "Level 3. 2200 experience (Learn the Steal expression)"). Used on an item on a shop counter; hold until the steal bar fills to take the item without being caught. Counts as a crime if seen.
- **Lockpick** — Learned at **Guile Level 6, which costs 27,400 experience** (per the same Steam Community "Fable TLC Experience Guide": "Level 6. 27400 experience (Learn the Lockpick expression)"). Used on a locked door to open it over a timer; counts as a crime if seen.

### Lost Chapters Additions
Expressions added in the 2005 expanded re-release.
- **Yeron** — Oracle of Snowspire quest. One of four "glyph" gestures; used at the Snowspire Oracle to ask about the People of Albion.
- **Moryk** — Oracle of Snowspire quest. Used to ask the Oracle about the Lore/Legends of Albion.
- **Calran** — Oracle of Snowspire quest. Used to ask about the Creatures/Beasts of Albion.
- **Avisto** — Oracle of Snowspire quest. Used to ask about the History of Albion. (The four gestures form the letters Y, M, C, A — a Village People "Y.M.C.A." reference — and can be used as a dance in town.)
- **Ballet Dance** — Extremely Good alignment (maximum good). A "fun"/good dance.
- **Cock-A-Doodle-Doo** (also "Chicken"/"Cock Call") — Won from the Chicken Kicking Competition mini-game in Oakvale (a Lost Chapters Bronze quest, available after the Treasure of the Ghost Pirate quest). A comedic chicken-flapping gesture.
- **Insult** — Moderately Evil alignment. Rude/offensive gesture; gains evil points. Per the Fable Wiki "List of Fable Expressions": "When Insult is unlocked, the Guild Master says 'You've Just Learned the Insult Expression' rather than 'You're now Evil enough to use the Insult Expression.'"

### Original Fable vs. The Lost Chapters
The base 2004 Fable shipped with the Social, Renown, Alignment, and Guile expressions listed above. The Lost Chapters added the seven listed in the section above (the four Oracle gestures, Ballet Dance, Cock-A-Doodle-Doo, and Insult). The expression system itself (categories, extendable meters, NPC rating effects) is otherwise identical between the two versions.

## Recommendations
- **To collect every expression in one playthrough is impossible without alignment swapping.** Plan two phases: push to maximum good alignment to unlock Crotch Grab, Disco Dance, Ballet Dance, Flamenco Dance, Thanks, and Apologise; then shift to extreme evil for Vulgar Thrust, Insult, Middle Finger, and Air Guitar. Donating at the Temple of Light or sacrificing at the Chapel of Skorm are efficient ways to swing alignment.
- **For renown expressions,** simply progress the main story and display trophies to crowds; ranks 1–5 unlock Sneer, Manly Arm Pump, Scary Laugh, Sexy Hero Pose, and Blood Lust Roar in order.
- **For the Lost Chapters expressions,** complete the Oracle of Snowspire quest (Yeron/Moryk/Calran/Avisto) and the Chicken Kicking Competition in Oakvale (Cock-A-Doodle-Doo, after clearing the ghost via Treasure of the Ghost Pirate).
- **For Steal/Lockpick,** invest experience into Guile to reach Level 3 (2,200 XP) and Level 6 (27,400 XP) respectively.
- **If you finish the game still missing some,** beat the final boss (dragon Jack of Blades) and do NOT skip the credits — remaining expressions are granted, and skipping prevents the save from registering completion.

## Caveats
- **No reliable source documents precise numeric values** for how much each expression moves the love/fear/funny meters in the original Fable/TLC. The effect descriptions here are derived from each expression's category (Social/Renown/Alignment/Stealth and the functional fun/scary/flirty/rude types) and from the documented general mechanics. Treat per-expression effects as directional, not exact.
- Some community lists differ slightly on edge cases — e.g., whether Flirt is a "beginning of game" or renown expression, and the exact alignment tier for Ballet Dance (maximum good) and Insult (moderately evil). The Fable Wiki "List of Fable Expressions" and GameBanshee's expressions page are the two most authoritative and largely agree.
- The "expression manuals/scrolls purchased from a vendor" premise does not apply to base Fable: The Lost Chapters — that is a Fable II/III mechanic. Per the Nexus Mods "Expression Vendor" page (nexusmods.com/fablethelostchapters/mods/126): "The mod adds a new trader in Darkwood Camp which sells expression books. Just like in Fable 2, these expressions are learned after purchasing the book. The list of new expressions is: Pray Clap Cough Kneel Seduce Levitate Fornicate Thyselves Slap Boast Pickpocket." These are NOT part of the original game.
- A handful of expression names exist only in the game's unused code (e.g., "Pray," "Pick Pocket," "Bandit Waves") and never made it into the final release; they are not playable in normal TLC.
