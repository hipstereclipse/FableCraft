# The forge

Everything you can wear or swing is craftable. 131 recipes cover the whole
chain, from folding your first steel to quenching a Master ingot in Will
Shards.

---

## Materials

The smithing chain is short and it makes Albion-sense.

| Material | How you get it |
|---|---|
| **Iron ingot** | Vanilla iron. The apprentice's metal. |
| **Steel ingot** | Two iron ingots and a coal, shapeless at a crafting table |
| **Obsidian ingot** | Smelt a block of obsidian in a furnace or blast furnace — volcanic glass drawn molten |
| **Will Shard** | Mine **azurite ore** |
| **Master ingot** | One steel ingot and **two** Will Shards — steel that remembers the magic |

### Azurite ore

Azurite generates in every overworld biome on the underground pass, nine
attempts per chunk, in veins of up to seven blocks, between **y 4 and y 54**.
It takes four seconds to mine and glows blue in the deep dark. It is the only
source of Will Shards, so the Master tier is gated behind going underground
rather than behind grinding.

## Weapons

Fifty-eight weapons: forty-eight melee and ten ranged.

### The tiers

Nine melee shapes repeat across four metal tiers — **longsword, katana,
cleaver, axe, mace, pickhammer, greataxe, greatsword, greathammer**:

| Tier | Made from | Augment slots |
|---|---|:---:|
| **Iron** | iron ingots | 0 |
| **Steel** | steel ingots | 1 |
| **Obsidian** | obsidian ingots | 2 |
| **Master** | Master ingots | **3** |

Bows bind from planks, string and a tier ingot across **oak, yew, ebony** and
**Master**, in longbow and crossbow forms.

### The uniques

Fourteen named weapons sit outside the tier ladder. They cannot take augments —
they are already what they are.

| **Arken's Crossbow** | 220 |
| **Solus Greatsword** | 190 |
| **The Bereaver** | 190 |
| **The Avenger** (bow) | 185 |
| **The Harbinger** | 180 |
| **Avenger** | 180 |
| **Dollmaster's Mace** | 160 |

## Augmentations

Eight augmentation stones, bound at the forge into a weapon's empty slots. A
Master-tier weapon takes three.

| Stone | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Sharpening** | +25% damage dealt by this weapon |
| **Piercing** | Ignores a portion of enemy armour |
| **Health** | Restores health with every blow |
| **Mana** | Restores Will energy with every blow |
| **Experience** | Earn bonus experience from each kill |
| **Lightning** | Shocks struck enemies with chain lightning |
| **Flame** | Sets struck enemies ablaze |
| **Silver** | Bonus damage against Balverines, Undead and the supernatural |

Bindings are permanent unless you use an **Augment Remover**, which recovers
the slot at the cost of the stone.

Route stones into the forge through the **Items** page of the storybook menu.

## Armour

Thirteen complete four-piece suits — helm, torso, legs, boots — plus six
standalone hats and helms.

| Set | Crafted from |
|---|---|
| **Apprentice** | leather and cloth; what you wake up wearing |
| **Leather (bright / dark)** | leather, dyed to taste |
| **Chainmail (bright / dark)** | chains |
| **Platemail** | steel |
| **Villager** | cloth |
| **Guard — Bowerstone / Oakvale / Snowspire** | steel and the town's colour wool |
| **Assassin** | dark leather and obsidian |
| **Fire Assassin** | the assassin pattern, forged hot |
| **Archon** | Master ingots and gold — the endgame suit |

The standalone pieces are the **Wizard Hat** (plain, bright and dark), the
**Pimp Hat**, the **Holy Warrior Helm** and the **Demon Helm**.

Armour carries three social statistics alongside its protection — **alignment,
attractiveness** and **scariness** — so what you wear changes how Albion reads
you, not just how much damage you soak.

Equip individual pieces or a whole suit at once from the **Clothing** page.

## Consumables

Fourteen, and several of them move your morality.

| Item | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Health Potion / Great Health Potion** | restore health |
| **Will Potion / Great Will Potion** | restore Will |
| **Resurrection Phial** | brings you back |
| **Ages of Might / Skill / Will Potion** | permanent attribute gain |
| **Elixir of Life** | permanent maximum health |
| **Apple Pie**, **Tofu**, **Red Meat**, **Golden Carrot Brew** | food, morally neutral to good |
| **Crunchy Chick** | food, **−25 morality** per one you eat |

## Training

Experience buys seven attributes, five levels each, spent from the pool that
matches:

| Attribute | Pool | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Physique** | Strength | +melee damage, +size. Your shadow grows. |
| **Health** | Strength | +max health |
| **Toughness** | Strength | +damage resistance |
| **Speed** | Skill | +movement and attack speed |
| **Guile** | Skill | +luck, +stealth, better trade prices |
| **Accuracy** | Skill | +ranged damage |
| **Magic Power** | Will | +max Will energy and Will regeneration |

Physique genuinely changes your silhouette, as it does in the original — a
maxed melee Hero is visibly larger than an apprentice.
