# Next Guild bridge comparison

This is an independently inspected next-pass audit, not authored GP23 geometry.
Original TLC images91147839,131726186 and689540613 show continuous broad side
parapets, rising profiles, pale inset/round decoration and capped ends with a
separate tall lamp. The current bridges have thin spruce-fence rails and lantern
gateheads. The photos establish those visible features, not exact bridge width.
URLs, accepted hashes and ignored audit-file hashes are in bridge-followup.json.

No broken current crossing was reproduced. Both13-sample centerlines x57..69,
eight local routes and seven complete gate paths pass. Two scratch full-cube
rail variants preserve these paths and the old conservative full-column bound,
but leave only1 block between the new inner faces. Two authored Skill bodies,
width0.672 each, cannot fit side by side in that cross-section. The old fence
renderer suggests a wider opening; its1.75-block gap is an illustrative model,
not a measured native Bedrock collider. Route-graph equality therefore cannot
establish unchanged physical clearance. Reject these full-cube variants.

Investigate a thin opaque parapet with an explicit horizontal collision footprint,
or separately survey deck and approach width before moving rails. Adjacent strips
include water, bank/path materials and a bollard; do not assume free widening
space. Preserve deck/slab approaches, both centerlines, complete routes, fixtures,
anchors, RNG and occupied-world construction. All source files and schematic
prototypes remain ignored in tmp/conformance/gp23-bridge-audit/. Native geometry,
movement, passing and appearance are unrun; no production output changed.
