# GP21 original Guild reference follow-up

One additional explicitly attributed TLC Guild archery image was downloaded and
inspected at full 1024 × 768 resolution. It brings the supplementary accepted TLC
corpus from 26 to 27 views. Six additional classic Guild exterior images in a dated
2009 forum post were inspected at full resolution; they remain **uncounted
corroboration pending exact-edition confirmation** and do not independently support
production changes. Fourteen new candidate images were downloaded; seven non-Guild
Steam candidates were excluded after pixel inspection. This was a bounded search,
not an exhaustive survey. Exact URLs, image/HTML SHA-256 hashes, dimensions and
individual limits are in [reference-followup.json](reference-followup.json).

## Firing-divider evidence

The new [Steam screenshot 141099874](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=141099874)
([direct image](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/1082264740983808145/FD6694CA87C1AF4855829F975D4DBDC4E6246106/))
shows the low timber barrier between the shooter's dirt platform and the target
scenery clearly. Uprights with shallow caps support thick horizontal timbers; a
central section has diagonal braces, with closely paired posts separating adjacent
sections. The visible wood is green-brown. A curved timber lip edges the foreground
dirt platform. Separate mountain/castle scenery stands at different depths in front
of the large painted mountain/conifer panel.

This is a **transverse firing divider**, not proof of full-length side rails. Its
ends, full perimeter and entry openings are outside the view. It does not establish
a complete circular range or five perimeter dummies. The known GP16 screenshot
689540321 was re-inspected and corroborates the divider and curved platform lip;
it is not counted again.

The saved Steam card matches app 204030, screenshot ID and exact direct image URL.
The detail page explicitly names *Fable - The Lost Chapters*, contributor
`badboy466`, and posting date `Apr 24, 2013 @ 1:30pm`; the classic pixels match this
attribution. Posting date is not a capture timestamp. Exact game build and
modification status remain unknown.

A feasible geometry candidate is a short low timber divider toward the targets,
with measured openings retaining the current target lane and circulation. Stock
spruce fences approximate the horizontal timber cue; they do not reproduce the
original diagonal braces, broad members or caps. Minecraft extent, axis alignment,
openings and material choice must remain explicit adaptations. The independent
geometry survey must decide whether such a divider is safe before authoring it.
The old x80/x92 side-rail feasibility fixture is not original-layout evidence.
